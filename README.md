## 🚀 Video & Multimedia CMS: Unleash the Power of PHPVibe

**PHPVibe** is an all-in-one video-sharing CMS that goes beyond standard video hosting—offering native **image sharing** and **music (MP3 & SoundCloud) streaming** capabilities. Built on lessons from **ZubeeTube (2008)**, **PHPVibe (2010)**, and **MediaVibe (2018)**, it provides a complete self-hosted multimedia framework.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🎥 **Seamless Video Sharing & FFmpeg Transcoding:** Automated video uploads, multi-quality conversion via custom FFmpeg commands, thumbnail generation, and media management—no manual coding required.
- 📺 **YouTube Support & Multi-Video Import:** Integrates with the standalone [YouTube Video Importer](https://phpvibe.com/youtube-video-importer/L0/) plugin to import videos by search query, playlist, or channel and assign them to specific owners and categories.
- 🖼️ **Social Image Sharing:** Dedicated photo upload and gallery features inspired by Instagram, WeHeartIt, and Pinterest.
- 🎵 **Dedicated Music Component:** Separate audio upload flow, organized playlists, and a sleek waveform audio player for MP3 and SoundCloud tracks.
- 👑 **Premium Accounts & Verified Badges:** Monetize via paid memberships (ad-free browsing & exclusive content) and assign custom verification badges to creators and user groups.
- 🗂️ **Smart Lists & Content Filters:** Built-in discovery filters across channels, videos, music, images, and user activity feeds.
- 💬 **AJAX Comments & Emoji Inserter:** Infinitely scrolling AJAX comments with an integrated emoji picker and YouTube comment importer.
- 🎨 **Intuitive YouTube-Inspired UI:** Familiar layout designed to boost user retention and lower bounce rates.
- 📝 **Built-In Blog & Static Pages:** Publish articles alongside essential compliance pages (Terms of Service, GDPR, Privacy Policy).
- 🤝 **Community & Social Engagement:** Subscriptions, follows, likes/dislikes, activity feeds, direct messaging, and external video embedding.
- ▶️ **Multiple Video Players:** Switch between **VibeJs** (customized Video.js), **JW Player 7**, and **Flowplayer** in one click.
- 💰 **Advertising & Monetization Ready:** Supports VAST video ads in VibeJs, pre-roll/overlay video ads, and static banner placements.
- 🔐 **Social Logins:** One-click registration and login via Facebook and Google accounts.
- 🔍 **SEO & SEF URL Structure:** Custom permalinks, hashed IDs, meta-tag prepending/appending, and search-engine-friendly routing.
- 🌍 **Multi-Language Support:** Create, edit, and import language packs directly from the admin dashboard.
- 🧩 **Modular & Extensible:** Theme customization, user role permissions, caching controls, and custom plugin architecture.

---

## 🗺️ Skill & Customization Roadmap

### 🟢 Beginner Level
- **Core Setup & Database:** Configure Apache/Nginx web server and MySQL/MariaDB database connections.
- **Basic Branding:** Customize site title, logo, SEO metadata, and user role permissions.
- **Media Operations:** Manage video formats, quality presets, and user profile moderation.

### 🟡 Intermediate Level
- **Theme Customization:** Modify templates and stylesheets to match custom brand aesthetics.
- **Performance & Caching:** Tune server settings and caching layers for faster page loads.
- **Third-Party & Monetization Hooks:** Integrate analytics, social sharing, ad networks, and subscription gateways.

### 🔴 Expert Level
- **Custom Plugin Development:** Extend core CMS functionality by building custom PHP modules and hooks.
- **Database & Scaling Optimization:** Optimize SQL queries and storage delivery for high-traffic media portals.
- **Security Hardening:** Enforce HTTPS, strict upload validation, and periodic security audits.
